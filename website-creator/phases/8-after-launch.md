# Phase 8: After launch

Goal: Google knows the site, all profiles tell the same story, and after three to four weeks it gets measured whether it works.

## Right after launch

1. **Google Search Console:** create a domain property. Google provides a TXT record. The user enters it in DNS (or the agent via the hosting MCP, after an OK). Then submit the sitemap and request indexing for the home page. Search Console later also shows which search terms the site appears for, how often it gets clicked and at which position it ranks. The site needs neither Google Analytics nor a cookie banner for that.
2. **Bing Webmaster Tools:** can be imported from Search Console. Bing matters because ChatGPT's web search relies on Bing, among others.
3. **Align profiles.** Transfer the fact sentences from `copy.md` to all profiles: LinkedIn (person and company page), YouTube channel description, Instagram, Google Business Profile, industry directories. The same everywhere: who, what, for whom, where, and the link to the site. Whatever is wrong on a profile, AI search engines repeat.

## After three to four weeks

Repeat the same measurements as in phase 2 and compare:
- Rankings for the terms in the keyword map (Search Console or DataForSEO `serp/google/organic/live/advanced`).
- Ask the three ChatGPT questions again (`ai_optimization/chat_gpt/llm_responses/live`). Is the site cited now? Is the brand described correctly?
- How many inquiries came in, and from where (the field "How did you hear about me?").

Result as a "Follow-up measurement" section in `research/keywords-geo.md`.

## What comes next

- **Subpages** for the research topics the home page can't cover. Each with its own main term, in the style from `DESIGN.md`.
- **Appear in best-of lists.** AI search engines like to cite lists such as "The best providers for …". Ask to be included or place guest posts.
- **Content that links.** Anyone with a YouTube channel, podcast or newsletter: a piece on the core topic with a link to the site strengthens both. Google also cites videos in AI Overviews.
- **Reuse the design system.** From `DESIGN.md`, you can build a skill of its own that creates proposals, presentations and more pages in the same style.

## Done when

- [ ] Search Console verified, sitemap submitted.
- [ ] Profiles updated with the same facts.
- [ ] Date for the follow-up measurement in three to four weeks is set.

That completes the process. Set the status file to "Live".
