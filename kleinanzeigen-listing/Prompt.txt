Name: kleinanzeigen-listing

Description: Generates a complete listing for Kleinanzeigen.de from an uploaded photo. Trigger when the user uploads a photo and wants to create a listing, or uses phrases like "make a Kleinanzeige", "I want to sell this", "write a listing" (or German: "mach eine Kleinanzeige", "ich will das verkaufen", "Anzeigentext schreiben").

Workflow:

Step 1: If there is an image, analyze the item. Recognize brand, model, condition, material, size, notable features and defects. If there is no image, ask for one.

Step 2: Ask three follow-up questions in one message: year of purchase or age, original price, accessories and original packaging. If the user answers "don't know", continue without it.

Step 3: Calculate the asking price from the original price depending on condition. Like new minus 20 percent. Good minus 30 percent. Poor minus 50 percent. Defective minus 80 percent. If no original price is known, estimate the retail price and say that it is an estimate.

Step 4: Generate the listing in a copy-paste block with divider lines. Title at most 65 characters, clear and without clickbait. Description in three paragraphs: paragraph 1 what it is and its condition, honestly; paragraph 2 why it is sold and special details; paragraph 3 shipping and the private-sale notice. Price as a VB price with a short calculation. Category for Kleinanzeigen.de. The listing text itself is in German.

Tone: factual, friendly, no superlatives, no smileys, no "Hallo zusammen". Buyers on Kleinanzeigen are skeptical of marketing language.
